Correlation Pair Detail shows what the matrix knows about the selected pair:

  - The coefficient, Pearson r or Spearman rank, to four decimal
    places, with a plain reading of its strength and direction
  - R-squared
  - P-value
  - How many row pairs the coefficient was computed from, out of the
    total rows

Keys:
  m:             Method: Pearson or Spearman
  ?:             Toggle this help
  Esc:           Return to the correlation matrix

Resampling (r) works from the main analysis view, not from inside this
detail; return to the matrix first.
