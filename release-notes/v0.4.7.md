## What's changed

- `|` in a q where clause changes meaning: `&` and `|` (and the words `and`, `or`) now parse right to left like every other q operator, so write `where (a > 10) | a < 5` where you wrote `where a > 10 | a < 5` before
- q's `&` and `|` follow q's rule that any value exceeds a null: `&` with a null is now null, even `false & null`, and `|` with a null gives the other side
- q's `&` and `|` give the smaller and the larger of two numbers, and the conditions after `where` filter in turn, so an aggregate in a later one is over the rows the earlier ones kept
- In q, division of whole numbers now gives a fraction, as in q (`7 % 2` is 3.5, not 3); a whole number next to an integer column stays an integer (`a + 1`, `a & 5`); and `not` of a number is whether it is 0
