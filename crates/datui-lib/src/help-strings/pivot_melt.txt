Reshape data between long and wide formats.

  Tab / Shift+Tab:  Move between the rows (↑/↓ too); in a picker: choose
                    and move to the next or previous row
  ← / →:            Switch Pivot and Melt (h/l too, outside text fields);
                    in a text field ←/→ move the cursor
  Space or typing:  Open the row's picker, narrowed by what you type
  Enter:            Apply the spec echoed above the footer
  Esc:              Close without applying; while a pivot is computed,
                    stop it and keep the form
  ? / F1:           Show this help (F1 works in text fields)

In the picker: ↑/↓ move, typing narrows, Enter or Space chooses. Where
several can be chosen, Space toggles a column in or out and Enter closes
the picker ("Enter Done"); Esc backs out of the picker alone and keeps
the toggles made so far.

Pivot: long → wide (index × columns → aggregate(values)).
Melt:  wide → long (index, strategy, variable/value names).
