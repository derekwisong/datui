## What's changed

q queries now follow kdb+ q exactly, so some queries change meaning:

- `&` and `|` (or `and`, `or`) work anywhere and read right to left like every other q operator. Put the left condition in parentheses: write `where (a > 10) | a < 5`, not `where a > 10 | a < 5`
- Conditions after `where`, separated by commas, filter one after another: in `where x > avg x, y > avg y`, the second average is over the rows the first kept
- On numbers, `&` and `|` give the smaller and the larger value, so `0 | x` floors `x` at 0
- With a null, `&` gives null and `|` gives the other value
- Division always gives a fraction: `7 % 2` is 3.5. Use `floor[...]` for a whole number
- `not` of a number is true when the number is 0
