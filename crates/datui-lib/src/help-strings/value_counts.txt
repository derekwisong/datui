How often each value of one column occurs in the view, with the query,
filters and drill-down applied. F at the table opens it on the current
column, the one underlined in the header: the column g went to, or else
the first column the scroll shows.

  ↑ / ↓ (j/k):      Move
  PgUp/PgDn:        A page
  Home/End:         First and last line
  ← / → (h/l):      The previous or next column
  Enter:            The rows holding the value, as a drill-down; Esc
                    there comes back here
  s:                Sort by count or by value
  a:                Count every row, when the counts are of a sample
  y:                Copy the counts as TSV: every value, its count,
                    percent and cumulative percent
  e:                Export the counts to a file
  Esc:              Back to the table; while every row is being
                    counted, stop that and keep the sample
  ? / F1:           Show this help

The summary:
  Rows, Distinct and Nulls for every column; Sum, Mean, Min and Max for
  numbers; Min and Max for dates and times. Distinct leaves out null. A
  sample shows no Sum: a sample's is not the column's.

What is read:
  One pass over the column counts every row. A remote view, or one over
  100 times the sample size (10,000,000 rows at the default), held in one
  Parquet or IPC file is sampled first instead, and the header says so:
  sample of 100,000 of 36,839,175 rows. a then counts every row. The
  sample size is [performance] analysis_sample_rows; 0 always counts
  every row.

The listing:
  The 1,000 most common values, then the rest summed on one line: other
  (N values). Nulls (∅) have a line of their own: ranked by their rows
  when sorted by count, last when sorted by value. Cum % runs down the
  list. The bar is the line's rows beside the most common value's;
  the other line has none.
