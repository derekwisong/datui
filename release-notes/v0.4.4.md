## What's changed

- Scrolling the table moves its lines with the terminal's scroll region and draws only the new line, about a twentieth of the bytes, which makes scrolling over SSH and in tmux much lighter; `display.scroll_region = false` turns it off
- Tables of long text, such as articles, logs or JSON, move a row as fast as tables of numbers: a cell reads only what it can show of its value, and a scroll formats only the rows it brings in
- Filtering the home screen in a directory of thousands of files takes a fifteenth of the CPU it did, and the files on screen are measured first
- Past the bottom of the page, a row down moves one row again, not two
