## What's changed

- The home screen stays fast in big folders: it only looks at the files you can see, so typing and moving around stay quick even with 50,000 files
- datui starts lighter, using about a third less memory: the home screen opens with no file picked and loads nothing until you pick one (press Down, or type to search)
- Smoother over SSH: holding a key or typing fast redraws the screen once, not once per key
- Pasting into a text field pastes everything at once, and a pasted line break no longer acts as Enter
- A network share or bucket that stops responding says `not answering` after 30 seconds instead of spinning; Ctrl+R tries again
- If the files of a cloud dataset are replaced while you have it open, datui offers to reopen it and keeps your query, filters, sort and drill-down
